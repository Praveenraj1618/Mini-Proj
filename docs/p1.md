When evaluating a **Legal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) System**, there are **3 key metric categories** to monitor. Here is a breakdown of the specific metrics and what they tell you:

---

### 🔍 1. Retrieval Quality Metrics (Vector Search)

These metrics measure how accurately ChromaDB finds the correct legal clauses before passing them to the LLM:

| Metric | What It Measures | Ideal Target | Value in [p1.md](file:///d:/UserData/Desktop/Mini-Proj-main/p1.md) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Hit Rate @ K** | Percentage of queries where the actual source page is inside the top-$K$ retrieved chunks. | **> 90%** | **100% (Pass)** |
| **Keyword Recall** | Proportion of mandatory legal keywords (e.g. *confidentiality*, *remedies*, *term*) successfully captured in the context. | **100%** | **3 / 3 (100%)** |
| **L2 Distance / Similarity Score** | Vector distance between the query embedding and the retrieved chunk embedding. *(Lower L2 distance = higher semantic similarity)*. | **< 1.0** | **0.79 – 0.81** |
| **MRR (Mean Reciprocal Rank)** | Measures how high up (Rank 1 vs Rank 5) the correct clause appears in search results. | **~ 1.0** | **1.0** *(Rank 1 match)* |

### 🛡️ 2. Generation & Anti-Hallucination Metrics (LLM Safety)

For legal contract analysis, hallucination prevention is critical:

| Metric | What It Measures | Target Standard | Implementation |
